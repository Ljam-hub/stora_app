**STORA — Mobile Retail Management and Community Commerce System**  
**Course & Academic Unit**: Software Development & Systems Integration and Architecture (SIA)

### 1. Purpose of the Study
You are invited to participate in the User Acceptance Testing (UAT) session for **STORA**, a mobile software platform designed to streamline inventory, Point of Sale (POS), credit ledgers, and online ordering for retail store owners and neighborhood consumers. The purpose of this test is to evaluate the usability, clarity, accessibility, and effectiveness of the mobile interface.

### 2. Participant Tasks and Time Commitment
During this session, you will be asked to perform 2 to 3 standard tasks on a test mobile device (such as scanning product barcodes, recording sales, browsing a store catalog, or placing a mock test order). The testing session is estimated to take approximately **10 to 15 minutes**.

### 3. Voluntary Participation and Right to Withdraw
Your participation in this testing session is entirely voluntary. **You may choose to stop participating at any time, for any reason, with zero penalty or explanation required.**

### 4. Data Privacy, Confidentiality, and Protection (RA 10173)
Under the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**, your personal privacy rights are strictly safeguarded:
- **Scope of Data Collected**: We record usability metrics including task completion time, navigation actions, user feedback, and observed interface friction points.
- **Anonymity & PII Masking**: No financial credit cards, real monetary transactions, or government identity numbers are collected. Any personal contact numbers or names provided during the test will be masked and de-identified in all published documentation.
- **Data Storage & Security**: All experimental observations and test records will be stored securely on encrypted drives and will only be accessible to authorized project evaluators.
- **Data Deletion / Right to Erasure**: You have the right to request the inspection, correction, or permanent deletion of your testing records at any time.
