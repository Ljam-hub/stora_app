OFFICIAL CONCEPT NOTE
STORA: Smart Inventory & Subscription Platform
Proponent: Team Stora | Target Sector: MSME Digitalization & FinTech | Year: 2026
I. SUMMARY
Stora is an offline-resilient, mobile-first inventory and sales-tracking application engineered specifically for micro and small enterprise (MSME) owners in the Philippines—such as neighborhood sari-sari stores, wet market vendors, and home-based online sellers—who currently manage daily stock using paper notebooks, memory, or complex spreadsheets. Functioning as an ultra-lightweight point-of-sale (POS) and inventory tracker, Stora enables store owners to register stock, scan product barcodes using their smartphone camera, automatically log cash and digital sales, and receive real-time low-stock alerts without requiring expensive dedicated hardware or terminals.
Stora follows a highly accessible freemium subscription model: micro-merchants enjoy a Free Forever Plan for up to 20 items with offline capabilities, accompanied by an initial 14-day Free Trial of the Unlimited Premium Tier upon registration. Once a business outgrows the 20-item tier, the owner upgrades for a flat ₱100/month subscription paid through GCash, verified via an admin approval workflow in the early stage and scaling toward automated payment gateway processing. Stora's core value is zero-friction digital transformation for entrepreneurs who cannot afford complex, enterprise POS systems.
II. BACKGROUND OF THE PROBLEM
Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) account for 99.5% of all business establishments in the Philippines and generate over 65% of total employment (DTI, 2023). Over 90% of these are micro-enterprises, led by approximately 1.3 million neighborhood sari-sari stores. Despite their vital socio-economic importance, the vast majority continue to operate through manual, paper-based methods ('talaan') or mental memory due to critical systemic barriers:
Frequent Stockouts & Lost Revenue: Without real-time inventory tracking, store owners do not notice stock depletion until customers ask for items that are already out of stock, causing preventable daily revenue loss.
Manual Reckoning Discrepancies & Pilferage: Paper notebooks are prone to miscalculations, water damage, and unrecorded sales, obscuring true profit margins and making periodic stock audits nearly impossible.
Inaccessible Enterprise POS Systems: Commercial POS platforms (e.g., Utak, StoreHub, Mosaic) cost between ₱15,000 to ₱40,000 in upfront hardware and charge ₱1,000 to ₱3,000 in monthly fees—virtually all requiring credit cards that micro-entrepreneurs do not possess.
Credit Invisibility: Without verified digital transaction histories, micro-retailers cannot prove business viability to banks, leaving them vulnerable to informal, high-interest lenders ('5-6').
III. PROPOSED STARTUP SOLUTION
Stora provides an integrated mobile software solution consisting of a cross-platform Flutter mobile application paired with a centralized Django REST administrative backend:
Smartphone Barcode Scanning: Eliminates manual typing by utilizing the mobile device's camera to scan UPC/EAN retail barcodes instantly.
Offline-First Architecture (Drift/SQLite): Guarantees that sales recording, cart checkout, and inventory browsing function reliably during power outages or mobile data disruptions, automatically syncing to the cloud when reconnected.
Automatic Stock Deduction & Alerts: Deducts stock in real time upon sale completion and triggers visual and push notifications when quantities breach reorder thresholds.
GCash-Native Payment Onboarding: Allows store owners to pay their ₱100/month subscription directly via GCash QR code, uploading screenshot proofs for rapid admin approval without credit card friction.
Store Location & Customer Discoverability: Includes store geolocations, allowing local community buyers to discover nearby open stores and view item availability.
Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs): Stora directly advances SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth) by improving micro-enterprise productivity and profitability, and SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure) by fostering digital financial inclusion for underserved MSMEs.
IV. OBJECTIVES
1. Digitize Grassroots Retail Operations: Provide an intuitive, zero-learning-curve mobile app enabling micro-retailers to catalog products and begin tracking sales in under 10 minutes.
2. Mitigate Stockouts and Shrinkage: Reduce stock calculation discrepancies and lost sales by at least 80% among participating pilot stores within 30 days of active use.
3. Establish a Cashless Grassroots Subscription Model: Validate an affordable ₱100/month GCash-based subscription model, achieving a 15% to 20% free-to-paid conversion rate among pilot MSMEs.
4. Deploy a Scalable Backend & Verification System: Build an auditable admin infrastructure capable of managing subscriptions, verifying payments, and transitioning smoothly into automated payment gateways (e.g., PayMongo).
V. TARGET MARKET & BENEFICIARIES
The primary beneficiaries are micro, small, and medium enterprise owners in the Philippines. The market sizing is structured as follows:
Total Addressable Market (TAM): ~1,300,000+ registered and informal sari-sari stores, wet market vendors, and micro-retailers nationwide.
Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM): ~450,000 smartphone-equipped, GCash-active micro-retailers in urban and peri-urban commercial centers.
Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM): 2,500 paying store subscribers within 18 to 24 months across initial pilot deployment zones (NCR, Region IV-A, and Metro Cebu).
Secondary beneficiaries include local neighborhood customers who benefit from reliable goods availability and transparent pricing, as well as regional wholesalers who can eventually access aggregated, anonymized demand data.

VII. BUSINESS MODEL
Stora employs a value-based freemium SaaS model designed for frictionless onboarding:
Free Forever Tier (₱0/mo): Supports up to 20 inventory items, offline cash sales recording, camera barcode scanning, and basic daily sales totals. Designed to eliminate adoption hesitation.
14-Day Full Premium Trial: New store owners receive 14 days of 20 items inventory and real-time alerts upon registration to experience full product value.
Premium Subscription Tier (₱100/mo or ₱1,000/yr): Unlocks unlimited inventory items, automated push notifications for low stock, sales trends, CSV/PDF bookkeeping exports, and store locator visibility.
Revenue Milestones: 100 stores = ₱10,000 MRR (Pilot validation); 1,000 stores = ₱100,000 MRR (₱1.2M ARR); 3,000 stores = ₱300,000 MRR (₱3.6M ARR at operational self-sufficiency).
VIII. MARKET ANALYSIS
The Philippine retail market is uniquely primed for Stora due to two macroeconomic catalysts: first, widespread digital wallet penetration with over 94 million registered GCash accounts and the national standardization of QR Ph by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP); and second, the DTI E-Commerce Roadmap 2024-2028 mandating grassroots merchant digitization. Micro-entrepreneurs who previously lacked formal bank accounts now use smartphones and e-wallets daily. By meeting merchants on their existing devices and payment methods, Stora eliminates the friction that caused past enterprise software initiatives to fail.
IX. OPERATIONS PLAN
The technical development and pilot validation span an 8-week agile development cycle followed by a 4-month live community pilot:
Sprint 1 (Weeks 1–2): UI/UX wireframes & design system, Django REST scaffold, JWT authentication, PostgreSQL database schema, and Drift/SQLite mobile local storage initialization.
Sprint 2 (Weeks 3–4): Product and category CRUD, integrated mobile camera barcode scanning engine, 20-item tier restriction enforcement, and cloud sync logic.
Sprint 3 (Weeks 5–6): POS cart checkout, automatic stock decrement, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) low-stock alerts, and GCash QR payment submission screen.
Sprint 4 (Weeks 7–8): Custom Django admin verification dashboard, offline sync stress testing, performance optimization on entry-level Android devices, and APK production deployment.
Months 3–6 (Live Pilot): Onboarding of 100 pilot sari-sari stores across 3 partner barangays, weekly merchant support, feedback clinics, and automated payment gateway sandbox integration.
6-Person Team Roles: Project Manager (Agile oversight & DICT liaison); UI/UX Designer (Accessible merchant interfaces); Mobile Developer (Flutter & camera/scanner); Backend Developer (Django REST & database); QA Tester 1 (Device & offline stress testing); QA Tester 2 (API security, latency & DPA compliance).
